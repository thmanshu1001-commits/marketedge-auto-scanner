How this scan works

  • Uses up to the last 20 completed daily candles returned by the data source.
  • Looks for at least 4 spaced reactions near a price zone, with close price near that zone.
  • Support: latest two candles red and older volume greater than newer volume.
  • Resistance: latest two candles green and older volume greater than newer volume.

This is a screening aid, not investment advice. Verify the chart and data before trading.
